P. Kumararatnam, J.

The above-named Accused-Appellants (hereinafter referred to as the

Appellants) with the 2nd Accused were indicted jointly in the High Court of

Ratnapura as follows:

1. On or about 05.05.1995 at Balangoda, within the jurisdiction of the

Ratnapura High Court the Accused together with the now deceased

persons namely R. M. Sunil Abeyranasinghe, A. A. Indika Gunasekara

and U. P. Gunasiri, being armed with hand grenades, knife and

firearms and having a common object of the prevention of the

enforcement of law, were members of an unlawful assembly, and

thereby committed an offence punishable under Section 141 of the

Penal Code.

2. At the same time, place and in the course of the same transaction,

one or more members of the said unlawful assembly fired at Police

Constable No. 21415 Athukorala with a gun and caused injuries to

him; and, if by such act the death of the said Police Constable No.

21415 Athukorala had been caused, the members of the said unlawful

assembly would have been guilty of murder, therefore the Accused

committed the offence of attempted murder, and the said offence was

committed in prosecution of the common object of the unlawful

Background of the Case.

According to PW1, SI/Karunaratna, when he was at a road barrier with other

police officers, a van bearing the number 54-9813 had arrived to pass the

barrier. When PW1 had signalled to stop, the van had defied his order and

had proceeded. The PW1 told PW2 PC 21415 Athukorala to stop the van as

he was standing away from the barrier. Obeying the signal of PW2 they had

slowed the van; however, the inmates had dragged PW2 into the van and had

driven off from the scene. PW1 had passed the message to the Balangoda

Police and they had stopped the said van near the Balangoda clock tower.

While approaching the van, the inmates of the van had opened fire at the

police and the police had retaliated by firing at the van. Two persons in the

van had received serious injuries and died of their injuries. The 1st Appellant

who was in the front seat had surrendered to the police. PW2, who was

kidnapped, had also received injuries. The 2nd Accused and 2nd Appellant

had been arrested by the police. The driver of the van had fled from the scene.

According to PW2, PC Athukorala, the Appellants had not done anything

while he was in the van.

The 1st Appellant giving evidence from witness box denied the charges

levelled against him. The 2nd Appellant also denied the charges when giving

evidence from the witness box.

Before the commencement of the argument, the learned President’s Counsel

and the Counsel for the 1st Appellant submitted to court that the Appellants

are only contesting the sentence. Considering the circumstances under

which the incident happened, the learned Counsel submits that this is an

appropriate case to impose a non-custodial sentence.

In this case, the Appellants and the other Accused were indicted for unlawful

assembly and committing attempted murder punishable under Section 300,

voluntarily causing hurt to deter a public servant from his duty punishable

under Section 323, Kidnapping with intent secretly and wrongfully to confine

a person punishable under Section 356 of Penal Code and Possession of two

hand grenades, possession of a gun, possession of ammunitions and

retaining a stolen property. After trial the Learned High Court Judge found

the Appellants and the 2nd Accused guilty only for the 1st, 3rd and 4th

Since all the Appellants and the 2nd Accused had been found guilty after a

full borne trial, the learned President’s Counsel who appeared for the 2nd

Appellant and the Counsel for the 1st Appellant submits to this court that

non-custodial sentences should have been imposed equally in this case, for

both Appellants.

The learned Deputy Solicitor General in keeping with the highest traditions

of the Attorney General’s Department too agreed that this is an appropriate

case to re-consider the sentence imposed by the Learned High Court Judge.

Both the Counsel jointly informed that the Appellants had been in remand

custody before and after the conviction, before they were granted bail.

Considering the submissions made by both the Counsel, I set aside the

sentence imposed on the 3rd count by the Learned High Court Judge and

substitute it with 02 years rigorous imprisonment with a fine of Rs.5000/-,

with a default sentence of 03 months simple imprisonment. I order the

sentences imposed on count number 01 and 03 to remain the same. Further,

I order the sentences imposed on count number 01, 03, and 04 to run

concurrently. Considering the submissions made by both parties, I further

order the 02 years rigorous imprisonment to be suspended for five years. The

Appellants have to pay the fine imposed by the High Court and this court.

The fines are subject to the default sentence.

Subject to the above variation in the sentences, the Appeal is dismissed.
