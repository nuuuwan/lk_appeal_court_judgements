Judgement

After the period of the earlier trustees of the Buhary Mosque and Bary Madrasa of Galbokka,

Weligama, the Wakf Board of Sri Lanka by its order dated 20.09.2023, appointed three trustees

for the Buhary Mosque and Bary Madrasa for a period of three years commencing from

20.09.2023 till 19.09.2026. Their appointments were confirmed. Two out of the three trustees

had passed away from the COVID-19 pandemic, and the remaining trustee was not permitted

to take over the affairs.

Thereafter, this was brought to the Wakf Board, and the Wakf Board order directed to inform

in writing to the person in-charge of the Mosque and Madrasa to hand over all the documents

and accounts, and all other possessions that belongs to the administration and the management

of the Mosque and Madrasa, to the new trustees.

However, there had been some commotions, and in the meantime, a complaint was made by

the single trustee to the Board on 13.12.2023 for an order to issue notice under Section 15A(1)

of the Muslim Mosques and Charitable Trusts or Wakfs Act, No. 51 of 1956 (as amended).

Upon this, notice was issued by the Board on 16.03.2024, and the Respondents, having

appeared, took up the position that the notice was against the law and that they will not

handover the documents and other relevant material to the new trustees.

When the inquiry was taken up, the present Appellants (those claimed to have been appointed

by the Jamaath) had filed the appeal that relates to the case bearing No. WKF/03/2025 and the

appeal case bearing No. WKF/02/2024.

However, after submissions, the Tribunal decided to dismiss the appeal made by the present

Appellant without further hearing by its order dated 26.04.2025.

7

Being aggrieved by the said order dismissing the appeal without going into the merits, the

Petitioners have filed this Appeal bearing No. WKF/03/2025.

The same objections were raised in respect of this Appeal as well by the Respondents.

Therefore, this Appeal should also stand dismissed for the same reasons that I have adumbrated

in my judgement in the case bearing No. WKF/02/2024.

Conclusion

For the reasons adumbrated above, I dismiss this Application, without costs.
