JUDGMENT

P. Kumararatnam, J.

The above-named Accused-Appellants (hereinafter referred to as the

Appellants) with five others were indicted jointly in the High Court of Jaffna

as follows:

1. On 01.11.2016 at Thunnalai, in the jurisdiction of Jaffna High Court

the Accused together with Arasan Rajeswaran, who is now deceased,

were members of an unlawful assembly, the common object of which

was to cause grievous hurt to Krishnapillai Kamaleswaran, thereby

committing an offence punishable under Section 140 of the Penal

Code.

2. In the course of the same transaction, the Accused together with

Arasan Rajeswaran, who is now deceased, committed attempted

murder on Krishnapillai Kamaleswaran thereby committing an

offence punishable under Section 300 read with Section 146 of the

Penal Code.

3. In the course of the same transaction, the Accused together with

Arasan Rajeswaran, who is now deceased and with Gunadas Robin

committed attempted murder and grievous hurt on Krishnapillai

Kamaleswaran thereby committing an offence punishable under

Section 300 read with Section 32 of the Penal Code.

4. In the course of the same transaction, the Accused together with

Arasan Rajeswaran, who is now deceased, committed grievous hurt to

Krishnapillai Ravigeethan an offence punishable under Section 317

read with Section 146 of the Penal Code.

5. In the course of the same transaction, the Accused together with

Arasan Rajeswaran, who is now deceased, committed grievous hurt to

Krishnapillai Ravigeethan an offence punishable under Section 317

read with Section 32 of the Penal Code.

The trial commenced before the High Court Judge of Jaffna as the Appellants

had opted for a non-jury trial. The prosecution had called 08 witnesses and

marked productions P1-P4. After the conclusion of the prosecution case, the

Learned High Court Judge had called for the defence and the Appellants and

the other accused had given evidence under oath and closed their case. After

considering the evidence presented by both parties, the Learned High Court

Judge had convicted the Appellants and the others for 1st and 3rd counts and

sentenced as follows:

• For the 1st Count each of the Appellants were sentenced to 03 months

rigorous imprisonment with a fine of Rs.5000/-. In default 45 days

simple imprisonment.

• For the 3rd Count the 1st Appellant was sentenced to 05 years rigorous

imprisonment with a fine of Rs.20,000/-. In default 12 months simple

imprisonment. In addition, the Learned High Court Judge had directed

the 1st Appellant to pay a compensation of Rs.200,000/- to PW1 with

a default sentence of 2 years simple imprisonment.

• For the 3rd Count the 2nd and 3rd Appellants were sentenced to 02 years

rigorous imprisonment with a fine of Rs.20,000/-. In default 12

months simple imprisonment. In addition, the Learned High Court

Judge had directed the 2nd and 3rd Appellants to pay a compensation

of Rs.150,000/- each to PW1 with a default sentence of 18 months

simple imprisonment.

Being aggrieved by the aforesaid conviction and the sentence, the Appellants

preferred this appeal to this court.

Although two other Accused were sentenced, they did not appeal against

their sentence.

The Appellants were present in court as they are on bail pending appeal.

Background of the Case.

According to PW1, Kamaleswaran, the injured person, on the day of the

incident, at about 10.00 am, upon hearing the voice of his brother

Raveendran, when he had gone near the gate of his house, he had seen the

Appellants and the other Accused heckling at his brother. When he had

inquired from them as to why they were heckling, the Appellants and others

had attacked him with knives. Due to the injuries he sustained, he had

received in house treatment at the Jaffna Teaching Hospital for about 10

days. On a previous occasion, PW1 was attacked with knives by the relatives

of the 2nd Appellant and for which he was hospitalised for about two days.

The cause of this continuous attack was due to a money transaction

involving the 4th Accused.

In the cross examination, PW1 admitted that a case was pending before the

Magistrate Court of Point Pedro against him for causing injuries to the 2nd

Appellant.

According to PW5, the JMO who examined PW1, a sharp cut injury on left

elbow which exposed the joint cavity and a laceration on the right palm were

noted. The JMO has also stated that the severity of the injuries is consistent

with that of what would be the result of injuries caused by a sharp cutting

instrument. The injuries are categorised as grievous injuries.

The Appellants giving evidence from the witness box denied that they had

assaulted and cut PW1.

The Learned Counsel for the Appellants had raised three grounds of

appeal which are set out below:

1. Has the prosecution proved the case against the Appellants

beyond reasonable doubts.

2. In view of the facts and the circumstances of the case, the

sentence imposed is unduly harsh and unjust.

As the grounds of appeal raised by the Appellants are interconnected, all

grounds will be considered together hereinafter.

At the commencement of the argument, the learned Counsel submitted to

court that the Appellants are only contesting the sentence. Considering the

circumstances under which the incident happened, the learned Counsel

submits that this is an appropriate case to impose a non-custodial sentence

with the enhancement of compensation.

In this case, the Appellants and the other Accused were indicted for unlawful

assembly and committing attempted murder and grievous hurt under

Section 317 of the Penal Code. Alternatively, the Appellants and the other

Accused were indicted under Section 300 coupled with Section 32 of the

Penal Code. After the trial, the Learned High Court Judge convicted

Appellants and the other Accused for the first count under 140 of the Penal

Code and for the third count under Section 300 read with 32 of the Penal

Code.

In this case, since the Learned High Court Judge convicted the Appellants

and others under Section 140 of Penal Code (as per the indictment) he should

have convicted the Appellants and others for the second count which is

under Section 300 read with Section 146 of the Penal Code. Since this defect

was not contested, I am not considering that defect in this judgment.

For the first count, the Learned High Court Judge had sentenced the

Appellants and the others for three months rigorous imprisonment with a

fine. For the third count they were sentenced differently.

The first Appellant was sentenced to five years rigorous imprisonment with

a fine of Rs.20,000/- and compensation of Rs.200,000/-.

The Second and third Appellants were sentenced to two years rigorous

imprisonment with a fine of Rs.20,000/- and compensation of 150,000/-.

The 4th and 5th Accused named in the indictment were sentenced to 21

months rigorous imprisonment with a fine Rs.20,000/- and compensation of

Rs.100,000/-. Further, the Learned High Court Judge had suspended their

21 months sentence for five years from the date of conviction.

Since, all the Appellants and the others had been found guilty after a full

trial, the learned Counsel for the Appellant submits to this court that all

should have been sentenced equally in this case. Hence, he invites this court

to consider this sentencing disparity and award relief to his clients subject

to the enhancement of compensation.

The learned Deputy Solicitor General in keeping with the highest traditions

of the Attorney General’s Department has agreed that this is an appropriate

case to re-consider the sentence imposed by the Learned High Court Judge.

Considering the submissions made by both the Counsel, I set aside the

sentence imposed on the Appellants and substitute it with the sentence as

follows:

For the third count, the 1st Appellant was sentenced to two years rigorous

imprisonment to run concurrent to the sentenced imposed on count number

one. Further, I order the two years rigorous imprisonment to be suspended

for five years. The 1st Appellant is fined for Rs.20,000/- with a default

sentence of 06 months simple imprisonment. Further, I impose a

compensation of Rs.400,000/- with a default sentence of 01-year simple

imprisonment.

For the third count, the 2nd Appellant was sentenced to two years rigorous

imprisonment to run concurrent to the sentence imposed on count number

one. Further, I order the two years rigorous imprisonment to be suspended

for five years. The 2nd Appellant is fined for Rs.20,000/- with a default

sentence of 06 months simple imprisonment. Further, I impose a

compensation of Rs.300,000/- with a default sentence of 01-year simple

imprisonment.

For the third count, the 3rd Appellant was sentenced to two years rigorous

imprisonment to run concurrent to the sentence imposed on count number

one. Further, I order the two years rigorous imprisonment to be suspended

for five years. The 3rd Appellant is fined for Rs.20,000/- with a default

sentence of 06 months simple imprisonment. Further, I impose a

Subject to the above variation in sentence, the Appeal is dismissed.
